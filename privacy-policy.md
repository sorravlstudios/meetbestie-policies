# Privacy Policy for MeetBestie

Effective Date: 1st May, 2025

At Sorravl Studios, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, MeetBestie ("App"). By using MeetBestie, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

This Privacy Policy is a part of and is to be read with the MeetBestie Terms of Use ("Terms"). The capitalized words used in this Privacy Policy but not defined herein shall have the meaning given to such words in the Terms.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

### Personal Information
- **Phone Number**: Required for account creation and verification.
- **Name**: Used to personalize your profile and interactions.
- **Age**: Used to ensure compliance with age-related regulations and to provide an appropriate user experience.
- **Gender**: Used to enhance personalization and matching features.

### Optional Information
- **Photos**: You may choose to upload a profile picture or other images. This is entirely optional and can be updated or removed at any time.

### Device Information
- **Device Model and Operating System**: Collected to ensure compatibility and improve app performance.
- **Unique Device Identifiers**: Used for security and analytics purposes.

### Network Information
- **IP Address**: Collected for security, fraud prevention, and analytics.

### Usage Data
- Information about how you use the app, such as interaction patterns, session duration, and features accessed.

### Communication Data
- **Calls Data**: Content of communications between you and other users. This data is stored in our cloud to prevent misuse and for police inquiry.

### Location Data
- **Location Information**: Derived from your GPS, IP address, or public posts that contain location information. This is used for location-based features and fraud prevention.

### Cookies and Tracking Technologies
- **Cookies**: We use cookies to enhance your experience, analyze app usage, and improve our services.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

- **To Provide Core Features**: Enabling audio and video calls, connecting you with new friends, and facilitating communication.
- **To Personalize Your Experience**: Tailoring the app experience based on your preferences and interactions.
- **To Improve Our Services**: Analyzing usage data to enhance app performance, fix bugs, and introduce new features.
- **To Ensure Security**: Monitoring and preventing unauthorized access, fraud, and abuse.
- **To Comply with Legal Obligations**: Ensuring compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
- **To Provide Location-Based Services**: Offering features that rely on your location, such as personalized suggestions.

We respect your privacy and do not share your personal information with third parties, except in the following circumstances:

- **With Your Consent**: If you explicitly agree to share your information with a third party.
- **For Legal Compliance**: When required by law, regulation, or legal process.
- **To Protect Rights and Safety**: If necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sorravl Studios, our users, or others.
- **With Service Providers**: We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating the app, such as hosting providers or analytics services.

## Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:

- Encryption of sensitive data during transmission.
- Secure storage of personal information.
- Regular security audits and updates.

### No Audio/Video Recordings
For your privacy and security, MeetBestie does not support audio or video recording features. All calls are conducted in real-time and are not stored on our servers.

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access and Update**: You can view and update your personal information within the app.
- **Delete Your Data**: You can request to delete your account through the app. Our team will review your data, and if there are no issues, we will delete your account within **7 working days**.
- **Withdraw Consent**: You can withdraw your consent for optional features, such as uploading photos.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Once your data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.
