# Refund and Coins Policy for MeetBestie

Effective Date: 5th June, 2025

At Sorravl Studios, we strive to provide a seamless experience for our users. This Refund and Coins Policy ("Policy") explains the terms and conditions related to the purchase, usage, and refund of coins within the MeetBestie mobile application ("App"). By purchasing or using coins in the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this Policy.

---

## Coins Usage

**Purpose of Coins**: Coins are a virtual currency used within the App to access certain features, such as:
- **Audio Calls**: 1 coin per minute.
- **Video Calls**: 2 coins per minute.

**Non-Transferable**: Coins are non-transferable and can only be used within the App by the account that purchased or earned them.

**Non-Refundable**: Coins are non-refundable once purchased, except as explicitly stated in this Policy.

---

## Purchasing Coins

**In-App Purchases**: Coins can be purchased through in-app purchases using the payment methods available in the App.

**Pricing**: The price of coins is displayed in the App at the time of purchase. All prices are subject to applicable taxes and fees.

**Final Sale**: All purchases of coins are final and non-refundable, except in cases of technical errors or unused services as outlined in Section 4.

---

## Coins Deduction

**Audio and Video Calls**: Coins are deducted based on the duration of calls:
- **1 coin per minute** for audio calls.
- **2 coins per minute** for video calls.

**Automatic Deduction**: Coins are automatically deducted from your account balance during calls. Ensure you have sufficient coins before initiating a call.

**User Responsibility**: You are responsible for monitoring your coin balance and usage. Coins will not be refunded for calls that were intentionally or unintentionally initiated by the user.

---

## Refund Policy

**Eligibility for Refunds**: Refunds or adjustments to your coin balance may be considered in the following cases:
- **Technical Errors**: Coins were deducted due to a technical issue or system error.
- **Unused Services**: Coins were deducted for a service that was not delivered or used.

**Reporting Issues**: To request a refund or adjustment, you must contact our support team within **7 days** of the transaction. Provide the following details:
- Date and time of the issue.
- Description of the issue.
- Relevant screenshots or evidence (if applicable).

**Review Process**: Our team will review your request and verify the issue. If the issue is confirmed, the deducted coins will be credited back to your account.

**Non-Refundable Cases**: Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:
- Coins were used as intended (e.g., for calls or other services).
- The refund request is made after the 7-day reporting period.
- Misuse or fraudulent activity is detected.

---

## Expiry of Coins

**Validity Period**: Coins do not expire as long as your account remains active.

**Inactive Accounts**: If your account remains inactive for **1 year**, your coin balance will be reset to zero. Inactivity is defined as no logins or usage of the App for a continuous period of 12 months.

**Contact Support**: If you have any concerns or disputes regarding coin deductions or refunds, please contact our support team at **contact@sorravlstudios.in**.

**Final Decision**: All refund and adjustment decisions made by Sorravl Studios are final and binding.
