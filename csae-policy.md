# Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy for MeetBestie

**Effective Date:** 5th June, 2025
**App Name:** MeetBestie  
**Developer:** Sorravl Studios

---

## Introduction

MeetBestie is committed to creating a safe and respectful community. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). This policy outlines our stance, detection, response, and prevention mechanisms to protect minors and comply with applicable laws and industry best practices.

---

## Zero-Tolerance Policy

MeetBestie strictly prohibits:
- The upload, sharing, or dissemination of child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
- Soliciting or engaging in any sexual conduct with or towards minors.
- Grooming or attempting to establish relationships with minors for exploitative purposes.

Any such activity will lead to immediate termination of the account and reporting to the relevant authorities.

---

## Age Restrictions

- **Minimum Age Requirement**: Users must be at least **13 years old** to use the app.
- Users under **18 years** must have **parental or guardian consent**.
- Any accounts found falsifying age or attempting to bypass these restrictions will be terminated.

---

## Detection and Moderation

To safeguard against CSAE, MeetBestie employs:
- **Real-time content monitoring** for profile photos and shared media.
- **Automated flagging systems** for suspicious behavior patterns.
- **Manual review teams** to assess reported users or content.
- **Content storage of communication data** (calls metadata) to assist with investigations, as outlined in our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

---

## Reporting Mechanism

Users can report any form of suspected child abuse or inappropriate behavior directly through the app. Reports are:
- Reviewed within **24 hours** by our moderation team.
- Escalated to law enforcement when appropriate.
- Handled with strict confidentiality and user protection in mind.

---

## Cooperation with Authorities

MeetBestie cooperates fully with law enforcement and child protection agencies. We may share:
- Relevant user data.
- Communication metadata.
- Other required information to support investigations.

---

## Education and Awareness

- Regular training for staff and moderators to detect CSAE.
- In-app safety reminders and parental control guidance.
- Collaboration with child safety organizations when needed.

---

## Consequences of Violation

Any violation of this CSAE policy will result in:
- Immediate suspension or termination of the offending account.
- Permanent ban from the platform.
- Reporting to legal authorities under relevant national and international child protection laws.
